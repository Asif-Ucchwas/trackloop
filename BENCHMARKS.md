# Benchmarks

Controller comparison with confidence intervals. Generated by `scripts/make_benchmarks_md.py` from `data/results/mc_summary.csv`, `mc_pairs.csv` and `mc_settings.json`; the results were produced at commit `d44377d` (clean tree: True).

## What was measured

- Nominal plant, every controller on every frozen path of its family at its design speeds.
- Seeds per family: road 150, parking 120. A seed fixes a start offset and heading error on each path (the same for every controller). The paths are a fixed set, so results hold for these paths.
- Quantity: RMS lateral error at the rear axle (metres). Each seed gives one number per controller and speed, the mean over that family's paths; the tables give the mean over seeds and a 95 percent Student t half-width over seeds. 34560 runs, 0 did not finish.
- Sizing rule: seeds were chosen so every cell's half-width is within 5% of its mean. Largest achieved: 4.9%; cells missing the target: 0 of 36. The sizing used the spread seen in the 5-seed benchmark. The first full run used 120 seeds for both families; four road cells (dynamic LQR and MPC at 10 and 20 m/s) were at 5.1 to 5.4 percent, so the road family was rerun with 150 seeds.
- Differences between controllers are taken seed by seed (same starts), which removes the start-to-start variation they share, so they are much tighter than the intervals on the means.

## How to read these, and what they do not say

- **Statistically distinguishable is not the same as important.** With this many seeds 36 of 36 paired differences exclude zero, the smallest being 0.3% of the error. Look at the relative column.
- **The intervals cover seed-to-seed variation only**, on these paths and with the placeholder vehicle and tolerance values. They do not cover controller tuning, a different vehicle, or different start ranges.
- **Means, not medians.** Earlier notes (`benchmark_road_note.md`) quote medians over runs. They can differ from the means here: the dynamic LQR at 20 m/s has a median of 0.034 m and a mean of 0.045 m, which suggests a tail of worse runs (not examined).
- **The 20 m/s road numbers for the dynamic LQR and MPC are partly outside the linear tire model's range** (front slip above the friction-limit angle in 10 of 20 runs for the LQR and 5 of 20 for the MPC in the 5-seed benchmark on the nominal plant, counted again with each model-error case in `docs/notes/root_cause_note.md`, section 5). See also `docs/notes/benchmark_road_note.md`.
- **Pure Pursuit and Stanley were not tuned for the road speeds.** I have not checked that their settings are fair at 20 m/s, so the size of the gap to the LQR and MPC there is not yet a statement about the controllers' designs.
- **Causes are given only where they were tested.** The worst runs (the Kinematic MPC's and the road MPC's failures under steering lag, the worst start on `road_curve_v20`, Stanley's chatter at 5 m/s) are explained in `docs/notes/root_cause_note.md`, with what each test does and does not show. No cause is claimed for any other difference in this file.
